### Reference mission (full reference greenhouse, 12 × 6.8 m, 3 benches, 32 stations, 384 plants)

State DONE; 32 stations; 46.82 m in 308 s; 1.392 Wh (analytic route model predicted 1.73 Wh / 373 s); 0 collisions; minimum clearance 0.093 m; stop error mean 34 mm, max 48 mm, 2.28°; localisation error 7 mm RMS; synthetic-truth flags: 24 true positive, 0 false positive, 4 missed (mild-severity plants). Path and occupancy map: `sim/results/reference/`.

### What the scenarios changed in the design

Failures during development are recorded because they are the useful output of simulation:

* **Turning envelope.** A collision in the 0.60 m aisle showed that the bumper corners, not the deck corners, set the envelope (R 280 mm, not R 255 mm). The mechanical calculation, footprint model and requirement R-MOB-05 were corrected.
* **Benches are invisible to the lidar.** Reversing and turning are now checked against the facility map, aisles are entered through an aligned way-point, and a narrow aisle is left forwards rather than by a U-turn.
* **Rotation next to an unmapped obstacle** swept a corner into it; a turn-envelope check on the scan was added.
* **Localisation loss** was detected too slowly; scan matching now runs at 5 Hz and motion pauses on the first poor match.
* **Blur metric.** Variance-of-Laplacian tracked noise rather than sharpness; replaced by edge spread on the reference card.
* **Wall-side 0.60 m aisles** cannot be entered with a 20 mm turning margin; requirement R-ENV-03/04 was re-scoped instead of tuning the test.

### Limits of the simulation

No wheel slip dynamics, floor unevenness, caster shimmy or mast flex; lidar returns are ideal (no glass, sunlight, reflective film, foliage); people are static boxes; the localisation map equals the world; synthetic plants are flat coloured ellipses; the power model is the calculation it is compared with. Nav2 and Gazebo were **not** run.
