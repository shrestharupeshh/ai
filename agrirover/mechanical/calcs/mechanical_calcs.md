## 2. Wheel loading (static, level floor)

The rover rests on the two drive wheels and the rear caster (front caster is shimmed 2.0 mm clear). Moments about the axle:

    N_rear = m g (-x_cg) / x_caster = 11.41*9.81*0.0203/0.190 = 12.0 N
    N_drive (both) = m g - N_rear = 99.9 N  ->  50.0 N per wheel (89 % of weight on the driven wheels)

## 3. Required wheel torque and speed

Wheel radius r = 50 mm, v_max = 0.5 m/s, a_max = 0.5 m/s², C_rr = 0.03, grade = 5 %, drivetrain efficiency = 0.85.

    F_roll  = C_rr m g cos(th) = 3.35 N
    F_grade = m g sin(th)      = 5.59 N
    F_accel = m a              = 5.71 N
    F_peak  = sum              = 14.65 N   (accelerating up the ramp)
    T_wheel,cruise = F_roll r / (2 eta)  = 0.099 N·m per wheel
    T_wheel,peak   = F_peak r / (2 eta)  = 0.431 N·m per wheel
    with safety factor 2:            0.861 N·m per wheel
    wheel speed at v_max = v / r = 10.0 rad/s = 95.5 rpm

## 5. Traction

* dry: F_traction,max = mu N_drive = 0.6*99.9 = 60.0 N; needed 14.6 N → margin 4.1x; traction-limited accel 4.96 m/s²; max climbable grade ≈ 27°.
* wet: F_traction,max = mu N_drive = 0.35*99.9 = 35.0 N; needed 14.6 N → margin 2.4x; traction-limited accel 2.77 m/s²; max climbable grade ≈ 16°.
* Wheel-slip torque on dry floor = mu (N_drive/2) r = 1.50 N·m, below the 2.65 N·m stall torque, so a blocked rover spins its wheels or trips the firmware current limit rather than stalling the gearbox indefinitely (current limit still required).

## 6. Braking / stopping distance

* bumper / E-stop input (MCU loop): latency 20 ms → d = v t + v²/(2a) = 0.5*0.02 + 0.5²/(2*1.5) = 93 mm (meets R-SAF-02 = 150 mm).
* lidar protective field (10 Hz scan): latency 120 ms → d = v t + v²/(2a) = 0.5*0.12 + 0.5²/(2*1.5) = 143 mm (meets R-SAF-02 = 150 mm).
* Controlled deceleration 1.5 m/s² needs mu ≥ a/(g·drive fraction) = 0.17 (< wet value 0.35 → OK).
* Braking torque per wheel = 0.34 N·m (< continuous gearbox limit).
* Uncontrolled coast after a hard motor-power cut depends on gearbox back-drive friction, which is **not published**; it must be measured (test T-SAF-03). The E-stop circuit therefore implements a controlled stop followed by a hardware-delayed power cut (see electrical docs).
* Forward pitch-over about the front caster would need a = g (x_caster - x_cg)/z_cg = 9.3 m/s² ≫ 1.5 m/s². The rover does rock onto the front caster (2 mm gap) when a > g(-x_cg)/z_cg = 0.90 m/s²; this is expected and bounded.

## 9. Structural checks

**Lower plate** (5052-H32, 4 mm), conservatively modelled as a simply supported beam spanning the two motor brackets (280 mm) with all supported mass as a centre point load:

    F = 85.5 N,  M = F L/4 = 5.99 N·m,  sigma = 6M/(b t²) = 4.9 MPa (yield 193 MPa → SF 40),  deflection = F L³/(48 E I) = 0.23 mm

**Motor output shaft** (Ø6 mm steel, wheel overhung 28 mm from the gearbox face, dynamic factor 2.5):

    F = 124.9 N,  M = 3.44 N·m,  sigma_b = 32M/(pi d³) = 162 MPa

  Bending stress is acceptable for shaft steel (yield typically > 400 MPa), but the **radial load rating of the 37D gearbox output bushing is not published by Pololu**. This is the main mechanical risk of the drivetrain; mitigation is an outboard pillow-block bearing (provision in the bracket hole pattern) if bushing wear is seen in test T-MEC-04.

**Camera mast** (2020 aluminium T-slot, cantilever 1070 mm, tip mass 0.55 kg, I ≈ 0.70 cm⁴ typical):

    1 g lateral shock: M = 8.3 N·m, sigma = 12 MPa (6063-T5 yield ≈ 145 MPa)
    stiffness k = 3EI/L³ = 1200 N/m; first bending mode ≈ 6.8 Hz
    tip deflection during 1.5 m/s² braking ≈ 0.7 mm

  A ~7 Hz mast mode means the camera rings after each stop; the mission planner waits `settle_time_s` (default 1.5 s) before capture and the image-quality gate rejects blurred frames.
