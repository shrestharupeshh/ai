# AgriRover GH-1 — electrical calculations

Generated by `electrical/calcs/electrical_calcs.py`. Estimates only; to be replaced by bench measurements (test T-PWR-01..04). Load figures marked *typ.* are typical values from vendor documentation or community measurements and are assumptions until measured.

## 1. Power budget by operating state (at the battery, W)

| State | Share of mission | Power [W] | Battery current @12.8 V [A] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| driving | 35 % | 18.7 | 1.46 |
| turning | 10 % | 18.2 | 1.42 |
| stopped_inspecting | 45 % | 14.3 | 1.12 |
| idle | 10 % | 9.8 | 0.76 |
| **mission average** | 100 % | **15.8** | **1.23** |

Drive power comes from the motor model in the mechanical calcs (cruise 3.7 W, turn 3.4 W) with a +50 % allowance. Motor current is **not** constant: ≈0.40 A per motor at cruise, ≈1.06 A accelerating up the ramp, up to the firmware limit of 3.0 A per motor in a stall, and 5.5 A × 1.22 = 6.7 A hard stall at a full battery if the limit failed.

## 2. Runtime

    E_nominal = 12.8 V × 12.0 Ah = 154 Wh
    E_usable  = E_nominal × (1 − 0.20 reserve) = 123 Wh
    runtime   = E_usable / P_avg = 123 / 15.8 = 7.8 h   (requirement R-PWR-01: ≥ 4.0 h → OK)

Pessimistic case (continuous driving, +30 %): 24.3 W → 5.1 h. The 20 % reserve also protects LiFePO4 cycle life and covers the return-to-dock leg; the firmware raises LOW_BATTERY at 12.0 V resting (≈20 % SoC, flat LiFePO4 curve - coulomb counting from the INA219 is the primary SoC estimate) and CRITICAL at 11.2 V.

## 3. Branch currents and fuse selection

| Branch | Typical [A] | Worst case [A] | Protection | Note |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Motor rail (VMOT) | 0.79 | 6.00 | inside F1 | firmware current limit 3 A/motor; TB9051FTG chops at ~6.5 A typ. |
| SBC buck feed (VSBC) | 0.85 | 2.73 | F2 = 5 A | Pi 5 + USB peripherals through the 5 V/5 A buck |
| Aux feed (VAUX) | 0.00 | 2.00 | F3 = 3 A | spare: lighting / USB hub |
| Logic 5 V (TSR 1-2450 input) | 0.07 | 0.56 | inside F1 | MCU, encoders, I2C sensors |
| **Battery total** | **1.71** | **11.28** | **F1 = 10 A** | battery BMS limit 20 A (reseller figure) |

* F1 (10 A mini blade) carries the 1.7 A typical load at 17 % of its rating. The arithmetic sum of every branch at its individual worst case is 11.3 A (113 % of F1): both motors stalled at the firmware limit while the SBC draws its full 27 W budget and the aux output is fully loaded. That coincidence is treated as a fault - F1 is allowed to open (blade fuses carry 110 % indefinitely and open in minutes at 135 %, in ~0.1-5 s at 200 %). A hard short (pack can source > 20 A) is cleared before the 2.5 mm traces overheat.
* Fuse coordination: F1 (10 A) > F2 (5 A) > F3 (3 A); wiring downstream of each fuse must be rated for the fuse, i.e. ≥ 18 AWG after F1/F2 and ≥ 20 AWG after F3 (chassis wiring, 80 °C PVC).

## 5. Voltage drop and dissipation in the power path

At I = 6.0 A (both motors at the firmware limit):

| Element | R [mΩ] | V drop [mV] | P [W] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| F1 10 A blade fuse (typ. cold resistance) | 8.0 | 48 | 0.29 |
| Q1 DMP4015SK3, Rds(on) at |Vgs| ≥ 10 V (max, hot) | 15.0 | 90 | 0.54 |
| R2 shunt 5 mΩ | 5.0 | 30 | 0.18 |
| Q2 DMP4015SK3 | 15.0 | 90 | 0.54 |
| PCB track 2.5 mm × ~90 mm, 1 oz | 19.1 | 115 | 0.69 |
| 3 × connector contact | 9.0 | 54 | 0.32 |
| **Total** | 71.1 | **427** | **2.56** |

* Drop is 3.3 % of the nominal bus - negligible against the motor speed margin.
* Each DPAK P-FET dissipates 0.54 W at 6.0 A; with ≈50 °C/W junction-to-ambient on a modest copper area ΔT ≈ 27 °C. Typical operation (≈1 A) is < 0.02 W. No heatsink required.
* Shunt: 0.18 W at 6.0 A, 0.50 W at 10 A, on a 2 W part. INA219 shunt voltage at 10 A = 50 mV → PGA ÷2 (±80 mV) range; LSB 10 µV ≙ 2 mA.
* Reverse battery: Q1's body diode blocks; the gate is clamped by D1. The TVS (D2) sits downstream of Q1 so it is never forward-biased by a reversed pack.
* 5 V logic rail: TSR 1-2450 (1 A, ~90 % efficient at 12 V in) supplies ≤ 0.35 A (MCU ≈ 50 mA, two encoders ≈ 2 × 10 mA, AMS1117 load ≤ 250 mA peak during SCD41 measurement); LDO dissipation (5 − 3.3) V × 0.25 A = 0.43 W peak, ≈ 0.1 W average on SOT-223.
